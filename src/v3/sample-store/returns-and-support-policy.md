# Linden & Co. returns, refunds and support policy

Version 4, effective 2026-10-01. Owner: Linden & Co. Customer Care.
Linden & Co. is an online store for home goods: bedding, kitchenware, lighting and small furniture.

## 1. Returns window
- Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Final-sale items (marked "Final sale" on the product page, including all clearance items) cannot be returned or
  refunded. Do not offer a refund or store credit for them.
- After 30 days we do not accept returns. Damaged or defective items are covered by section 3 instead.

## 2. Return shipping
- Customers pay return shipping, $8.95, deducted from the refund, unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the
  wrong item. Never promise free return shipping in other cases.
- Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.

## 3. Damaged or defective items
- Report damage within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We send a replacement or refund, and we pay shipping.
- Mattresses and furniture over 70 lb are not shipped back: we arrange a pickup instead.

## 4. Discounts and price adjustments
- One promo code per order. Promo codes cannot be combined and cannot be applied after an order is placed.
- We match our own lower price within 14 days of purchase, once per order. We do not match other retailers' prices.

## 5. Orders and delivery
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the customer must return the item.
- Standard delivery takes 4-8 business days. We ship to the 48 contiguous US states only.

## 6. Accounts and privacy
- Support never asks for a full card number or a password.
- Requests to delete personal data go to the privacy form at lindenandco.example/privacy. The assistant cannot delete
  data and must not say data was deleted.

## 7. When to hand off to a person
Hand the conversation to a human agent when the customer asks for one, for any order over $1,000, for legal threats
or chargebacks, and for anything this policy does not cover.
