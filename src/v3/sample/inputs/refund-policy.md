# Tallyfox billing, refund and account policy

Version 3, effective 2026-09-01. Owner: Tallyfox Customer Support.
Tallyfox makes invoicing software and card readers for small businesses.

## 1. Duplicate or incorrect charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the support assistant may refund the duplicate charge
immediately. Refunds reach the card in 5-10 business days. Always state that time frame.

## 2. Refund window
- Within 30 days of purchase: full refund to the original payment method.
- After 30 days: no cash refund. Offer store credit for the purchase amount instead.
- The assistant must never say a cash refund was issued for a purchase older than 30 days.

## 3. Subscriptions
Customers may cancel their Tallyfox plan at any time. Confirm the cancellation and that no further charges
will be made. Partial months are not refunded.

## 4. Accounts and privacy
- Password resets: send the customer to tallyfox.example/reset. Support never asks for a password.
- Data deletion requests: send the customer to the privacy request form at tallyfox.example/privacy.
  The assistant cannot delete data itself and must never claim the data has been deleted.

## 5. Shipping card readers
We ship card readers to the United States and Canada. Delivery takes 3-7 business days.

## 6. When to hand off to a human
Hand off to a human support agent when the customer asks for one, for warranty questions about card readers
(the assistant has no warranty information), and for anything this policy does not cover.
